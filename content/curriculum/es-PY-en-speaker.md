# Curriculum map — `es-PY>en-speaker` (English speaker learning Paraguayan Spanish)

**Status:** owner-editable pass-1 output. This is the input to pass 2 of
`content/prompts/curriculum-generation.md` and MUST NOT change once lesson generation starts.

**Provenance:** drafted by Gemini 3.6 Flash (pass 1), then edited for pedagogy against PLAN.md
§9 Q11 and the pass-1 constraints in the prompt pack. Gemini's Paraguayan vocabulary and can-do
statements are kept nearly verbatim — that is where it added real value. The *sequencing* is
Fable's spec, not Gemini's.

**Learner:** the owner. Priority per PLAN.md §9 Q9: grammar accuracy + listening comprehension
(`accuracy_focus`). Target outcome: functional independence, ≈ CEFR B1–B2 (§9 Q11).

**Dialect:** Paraguayan Spanish — voseo (`vos tenés`, `¿cómo te llamás?`, `sos`), local
vocabulary (tereré, chipa, yuyos), Guaraní loanwords where genuinely common in speech.

## What the edit changed, and why

The prompt pack requires **A1 = positions 1–12 reaching survival competence: greetings,
self-introduction, asking for repetition/clarification, numbers/prices, food, transport, basic
health.** Gemini returned a tidy 10/10/10/10 split that dropped three of those seven:

| Change | Reason |
|---|---|
| A1 widened 10 → 12 | The pack says positions 1–12. 10/10/10/10 is a model tidying, not a syllabus. |
| **Added #2 `clarification`** | Explicitly required by the pack and absent from all 40 of Gemini's rows. Without it a learner's first real conversation dead-ends the moment they mishear. Spelling your name aloud (`deletreame`) folds in here, which is why no separate lesson is needed before #28's paperwork. |
| **Added #3 `numbers-and-prices`** | Also required, also missing. Gemini's #3/#6 asked for prices in guaraníes without ever teaching the number system — and PY prices run in thousands with slang (`mil`, `luca`, `palo`). Teaching it before the two shopping lessons, not after. |
| **Added #9 `basic-health`** (survival subset) | The pack requires basic health in A1; Gemini's first health content was position 12, in A2. #16 keeps the fuller pharmacy lesson. |
| `home-and-neighborhood` A1 → A2 (#14) | Negotiating a lease is not survival competence; it displaced something that is. |
| `car-breakdown` merged into B2 #40 | Overlapped #22 (technicians) and #24 (narrating incidents); the surviving distinct content is the roadside/checkpoint situation. |
| Cut `real-estate-contract-discussions` | Subsumed by #14 (renting) and #28 (notarial paperwork). |
| Cut `analyzing-economic-and-social-issues` | Café-debate fluency is the least load-bearing item for "functional independence"; first to go when B2 tightened. |
| Cut `community-involvement-and-advocacy` | Same reason. Both are good C1 candidates once A1/A2 are proven in real use (§9 Q4). |
| WhatsApp/voice-notes kept as **one A2 lesson** (#21), not a theme | Standard CEFR "using the telephone". It is not in the pack's A1 list, so it does not displace anything there. |

Level split is now **A1 12 / A2 12 / B1 9 / B2 7 = 40** — front-loaded, because §9 Q4 says write
and prove A1+A2 before B1+ is worth writing at all.

## Before generating B1 content

The map introduces subjunctive (#27, #30), preterite-vs-imperfect contrast (#24), and the
conditional (#33). `ES_TAXONOMY` in `scripts/seed.ts` must carry matching keys or the tutor
files those errors under `other` and the dashboard never aggregates them (PLAN.md §10 item 3).
Added in the same commit as this file: `preterite-vs-imperfect`, `subjunctive-wrong-trigger`,
`conditional-forms`.
